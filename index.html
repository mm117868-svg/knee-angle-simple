Getting a useful reading
  1. Stand your iPhone or Mac securely to one side. The knee you select should face the camera. Keep one person in the picture.
  2. Allow camera access when asked. On iPhone, open this page in Safari. On Mac, use Safari or Chrome.
  3. Use good light and keep your hip, knee and ankle uncovered and visible. The orange line shows the measured leg; the pale lines show the rest of your skeleton.
  4. Move within a comfortable range. The live angle uses a short average to reduce jitter. Stop the camera before moving the device.

What the number means: this is an approximate knee-bend angle from the side-on camera image. A straight leg is 0°; a right-angle bend is 90°. It cannot distinguish bending from hyperextension or measure clinical extension lag. Camera position, clothing and hidden joints affect the estimate.

Prototype: these camera estimates have not been clinically validated. They are not a diagnosis, recovery score or replacement for a physiotherapist's measurement. Saved readings last only until you reload this page.
